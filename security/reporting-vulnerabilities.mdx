---
title: "Reporting vulnerabilities"
description: "How to report suspected P-BitM vulnerabilities privately and safely."
---

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, or
pull requests. Email
[GiacoLenzo2109@proton.me](mailto:GiacoLenzo2109@proton.me) and include:

- the affected component and version or commit;
- the conditions required to reproduce the issue;
- a minimal proof of concept using non-sensitive test data;
- the expected security impact;
- any suggested mitigation.

<Warning>
Do not include real credentials, captured data, production targets, tracking
identifiers, browser profiles, or personal information.
</Warning>

Security fixes are applied to the latest version on the default branch.
Issues limited to the intended privileges and current session of an already
authorized administrator, self-XSS, and cosmetic defects without security
impact are normally out of scope.

The [repository security policy](https://github.com/P-BitM-Framework/P-BitM/security/policy)
is the canonical source for current scope and submission requirements.
